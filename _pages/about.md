I am a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I work on natural language processing and machine learning under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research sits at the intersection of large language model reasoning, vision-language model hallucination, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

Academic Background
======

* **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024 &ndash; Present. Supervisor: Professor Junxian He.
* **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020 &ndash; 2024. Graduated in June 2024.
* **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
