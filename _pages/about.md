## Research Interests

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Exploring reasoning capabilities and reinforcement learning techniques for large language models
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Investigating the perception bottleneck and hallucination phenomena in VLCs
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Studying the truthfulness mechanisms and interpretability of large language models
